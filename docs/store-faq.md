# Store FAQ

## Who we are

Tamp & Bloom is an online shop for home-barista equipment: espresso machines, grinders, kettles and accessories. Tamp & Bloom is a fictional business used for this demo.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and bank transfer. Bank-transfer orders ship once the payment clears, which takes 1–2 business days. You can split a payment over 4 months with our instalment option on orders above $300.

## Contact and support hours

The support assistant on our website answers questions any time. Our human support team works Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 6 pm Gulf Standard Time, and replies to tickets within one business day.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are digital, come in $25, $50 and $100 amounts, and are valid for 3 years. They can't be exchanged for cash.

## Showroom

We don't have a physical showroom. Orders can't be collected in person.

## Bean subscription

Our bean subscription sends 500 g of freshly roasted beans every two or four weeks. You can pause, skip or cancel it at any time from your account page, up to 2 days before the next roast date.
