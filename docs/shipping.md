# Shipping

Tamp & Bloom ships from our warehouse in Dubai. Orders placed before 2 pm (Gulf Standard Time) on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, or on a Friday or public holiday, leave the next business day.

## Delivery times and costs

| Destination | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| UAE | 1–2 business days, free over $75, otherwise $6 | Same day in Dubai if ordered before 11 am, $12 |
| Other GCC countries | 3–5 business days, $15 | 2 business days, $35 |
| Europe and UK | 6–9 business days, $29 | 3–4 business days, $59 |

Standard delivery inside the UAE takes 1–2 business days, and standard shipping inside the UAE is free on orders over $75.

We don't ship to the United States, Canada or Australia at the moment.

## Tracking your order

When your order leaves the warehouse you get an email with the carrier name and a tracking number. Tracking usually starts showing movement within 12 hours. You can also ask our support assistant for the status of an order: have your order number (it starts with TB-) and the email address you ordered with ready.

## Customs and duties

Prices on the website include UAE VAT. Shipments to the UK and Europe are sent with duties unpaid, so the carrier may ask you to pay import VAT and duties on delivery. We can't refund duties you paid if you later return the order.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address until the order leaves the warehouse. After that, contact the carrier directly with your tracking number.
