# Bloom One espresso machine: care and troubleshooting

The Bloom One is our single-boiler espresso machine with a 58 mm portafilter and a 2-litre water tank.

## Warm-up

Switch the machine on 20 minutes before your first shot so the group head is fully heated. The ready light stops blinking when the boiler reaches brewing temperature, but the group head needs the extra time.

## Descaling

Descale the Bloom One every 2 months. If your water is hard (above 120 ppm), descale every month. Use only citric-acid descaler, run the descale program from the settings menu, and rinse with two full tanks of fresh water afterwards. Skipping descaling can void the warranty for scale-related faults.

## Backflushing

Backflush with the blind basket and a cleaning tablet once a week if you make more than two drinks a day. Backflush with plain water after every session.

## Steam wand

Purge the steam wand for 2 seconds before and after steaming milk and wipe it straight away with a damp cloth. If the tip clogs, soak it in hot water for 10 minutes and clear the holes with the pin supplied in the box.

## Error codes

- **E1: water tank empty.** Refill the tank and make sure it clicks into place.
- **E2: boiler overheated.** Switch the machine off, let it cool for 30 minutes, and switch it on again. If E2 comes back, open a support ticket.
- **E3: low pump pressure.** Usually the grind is too fine or the machine needs descaling. Coarsen the grind by two steps and descale. If E3 continues after descaling, open a support ticket.

## Brewing pressure

The pump is set to 9 bar at the group head. The pressure gauge should read between 8 and 10 bar during a shot. A reading above 11 bar means the grind is too fine.
