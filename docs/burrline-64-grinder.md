# Burrline 64 grinder: settings and cleaning

The Burrline 64 is our flat-burr grinder with 64 mm steel burrs and stepless adjustment from 0 to 50.

## Grind settings

| Brew method | Starting setting |
|---|---|
| Espresso | 8–14 |
| Moka pot | 16–20 |
| Pour-over (V60, Kalita) | 30–38 |
| French press | 42–48 |

Change the setting only while the motor is running, so beans don't jam between the burrs. Start in the middle of the range and adjust by taste: sour espresso usually means grind finer, bitter espresso means grind coarser.

## Cleaning the burrs

Clean the burrs every two weeks if you grind every day. Run one capful of grinder-cleaning pellets through the grinder, then grind 20 g of old beans to clear the pellet dust. Once a month, unplug the grinder, remove the top burr with the tool in the box, and brush both burrs dry. Never wash burrs with water, because they rust.

## Retention

The Burrline 64 keeps less than 0.3 g of grounds inside after a grind. Tap the bellows on top twice after grinding to push out what's left.

## Replacing burrs

The burrs last roughly 500 kg of coffee. Replacement burr sets cost $69 and are not covered by the warranty, because burrs wear out with normal use.
