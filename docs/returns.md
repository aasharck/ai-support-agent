# Returns and refunds

## Return window

You can return unused equipment within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories.

## Opened grinders and machines

Grinders and espresso machines that have been used can be returned within 14 days of delivery if they show no damage. We charge a 15% restocking fee on used machines and grinders because they have to be cleaned and inspected before resale.

## Items we can't take back

For hygiene reasons we can't accept returns of coffee beans, opened cleaning tablets, opened descaler, or gaskets and other seals once they've been fitted.

## How to start a return

Ask our support assistant to open a return ticket, or email returns@tampandbloom.example with your order number. We send a prepaid return label within one business day. Return shipping is free inside the UAE. For returns from other countries the customer pays return shipping.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the return reaches our warehouse. Store credit is issued within 1 business day if you prefer it.

## Damaged on arrival

If something arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and the box. Damaged-on-arrival items are replaced or refunded at no cost, including shipping.
