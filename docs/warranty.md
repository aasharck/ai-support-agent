# Warranty

## Coverage

All espresso machines sold by Tamp & Bloom come with a 2-year warranty. Grinders and kettles come with a 1-year warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date and covers defects in materials and workmanship.

## What isn't covered

- Burrs, gaskets, shower screens and other parts that wear out with normal use.
- Damage from limescale. Machines that haven't been descaled as described in the care guide aren't covered for scale-related faults.
- Damage from dropping the product, using it with the wrong voltage, or repairs done by anyone other than Tamp & Bloom.

## How to make a warranty claim

Open a support ticket with your order number, the serial number (on the sticker under the drip tray or on the base of the grinder), and a short video that shows the problem. A technician replies within 2 business days. If the fault is covered, we repair or replace the product and pay shipping both ways.

## Extended warranty

You can add a third year of warranty on espresso machines for $49 within 30 days of delivery. It isn't available for grinders or kettles.
